## Quick Answer: How much does a yacht charter cost in Tampa Bay?

A private yacht charter in St. Petersburg, FL is billed at one all-inclusive rate that covers captain, crew and fuel. The rate is $500/hr on weekdays (Mon-Thu, $2,000 for 4 hours) and $600/hr on weekends (Fri-Sun, $2,400 for 4 hours). Overnight and Bahamas trips start at $9,500+. Charters run aboard a 52ft Marquis Flybridge with capacity for up to 13 guests. A second yacht, Sly Fox (3 staterooms / 2 baths, sleeps 6), charters at $650/hr on weekdays and $750/hr on weekends, with multi-day Gulf Coast trips from $5,000. Yacht Away Now holds 133 verified Google reviews with a 5.0-star average.

## Deposit & Booking

- **50% deposit** locks in your date
- Remaining 50% balance due 7 days before departure
- Book 2-3 weeks in advance (4-6 weeks for holidays)
- Guest-initiated reschedules are accommodated within available dates
- If the captain calls off a charter for weather or safety, you pick any open future date and the booking stays valid for 12 months
