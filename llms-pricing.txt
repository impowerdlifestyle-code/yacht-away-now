## Quick Answer: How much does a yacht charter cost in Tampa Bay?

A private yacht charter in St. Petersburg, FL is one all-inclusive rate — captain, crew, and fuel included: $500/hr on weekdays (Mon-Thu, exactly $2,000 for 4 hours) and $600/hr on weekends (Fri-Sun, exactly $2,400 for 4 hours). Overnight and Bahamas trips start at $5,000+. All charters are aboard a 52ft Marquis Flybridge with capacity for up to 13 guests.
