## Quick Answer: How much does a yacht charter cost in Tampa Bay?

A private yacht charter in St. Petersburg, FL starts at $400/hr on weekdays ($2,200 for a 4-hour charter with fuel and captain). All-inclusive weekend packages are $2,000 for 4 hours or $2,500 for 5 hours with captain, crew, and fuel included. Overnight and Bahamas trips start at $5,000+. All charters are aboard a 52ft Marquis Flybridge with capacity for up to 13 guests.
