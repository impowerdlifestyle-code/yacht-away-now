🛥️ What to expect

  • Departure: St. Petersburg Municipal Marina, Slip 412 (Salty Hours — 38' Sea Ray, navy hull). Arrive by 4:15 PM for introductions, a safety walkthrough, and drinks before push-off at 4:30.
  • Charter length: 4 hours, returning to the slip around 8:30 PM. Route is flexible — we'll shape the day around wind, tide, and your group's vibe.
  • Onboard: I run the boat. Deckhand Marcus handles drinks, swim ladder, and music. Your only job is to relax.
  • Cell service: Strong everywhere we cruise. Post the photos.

🎒 What to bring

  • Swimsuit (wear it under your clothes — saves changing time)
  • One towel per guest
  • Reef-safe sunscreen (no aerosol or oil-based — they stain the deck)
  • Hat + polarized sunglasses
  • Light cover-up or hoodie for the post-sunset ride back
  • Cash for crew gratuity — 10–15% is standard, 100% goes to Marcus and me
  • A speaker or Bluetooth playlist — we've got audio, but bring your soundtrack
  • Any food or drinks you want. We'll have waters, ice, koozies, and a charcuterie board ready. BYOB welcome.

🚫 What to leave behind

  • No glass bottles — Coast Guard rule. We'll transfer to plastic dockside.
  • No hard-soled shoes — barefoot or soft soles only. Decks scratch easily.
  • No red wine on the white cushions, please 🙏

🐬 What you'll see

  • Bottlenose dolphins — we see pods on 95%+ of charters. They surf the wake.
  • Three Rooker Bar — sandbar 25 minutes out. Waist-deep water, perfect anchoring spot for the photo your crew will share for the next year.
  • Sunshine Skyway Bridge at golden hour — we time the return so you're crossing under it as the sun drops.
  • Manatees, sea turtles, ospreys, the occasional ray — bonus rounds.
  • Pass-A-Grille and Shell Key from the water — locals know, tourists don't.

✨ How to make it the best night of your trip

  1. Hydrate the night before. Sun + saltwater + drinks = next-level dehydration.
  2. Eat a real meal beforehand. Try Parkshore Grill — 5 minutes from the marina.
  3. You picked the right slot. Sunset is the move. Best light, dolphins most active.
  4. Bring water shoes for the sandbar — there are shells.
  5. Pace the alcohol the first hour. Four hours is long and the water is hot.
  6. Anniversary toast: Mark mentioned a moment. Tell me when, and I'll cut the engine in a quiet spot — Marcus will pour the champagne.
  7. Get there early. Marina parking fills up Saturdays. 4:15 PM is on time.
