## Tips

- **Always include a photo** — posts with photos get 10x more engagement
- **Always include a CTA button** with a link to a DIFFERENT page each week (this forces Google to crawl those pages)
- **Keep text under 300 words** — GBP truncates longer posts
- **Post on Monday or Tuesday morning** for best visibility
- **Use the Event post type** for holiday charters (July 4th, NYE, Gasparilla)
