The Beginning

How It All Started

Our founder, Josh Wilson, grew up on the waters of Florida's Gulf Coast. Weekends meant fishing trips with his father, afternoons spent exploring barrier islands by boat, and long summers where the horizon line felt like the edge of the world. That love for the water never faded — it only deepened.

After years in the marine industry, our founder saw a gap in the Tampa Bay charter market. Most operations treated guests like numbers — rotating passengers through cookie-cutter tours on crowded party boats with little regard for the experience itself. There was no real premium option. He knew there was a better way.

So he started with one yacht. A 52ft Marquis Flybridge, hand-selected for its three decks of space, its 600-mile range, and the kind of presence that makes a sunset cruise feel like an event. The goal wasn't to scale fast — it was to learn how to deliver this experience the right way, charter by charter, guest by guest.

That's still the foundation. But the vision goes further. Yacht Away Now is becoming a platform — a hand-curated collection of premium yachts from like-minded owners who care about the experience they're handing their guests. Owners who want their vessel to be a destination, not a commodity.

This is not a directory. This is not a marketplace. There are no jet skis, no pontoons, no rentals-by-the-hour mixed in. Every vessel on Yacht Away Now is private, premium, and operated to the same standard our founder set on day one — because that's the only kind of yacht we'll add. If you're an owner who shares that bar, we want to talk.

Today we operate from St. Petersburg, Florida, serving guests from across Tampa Bay and beyond. Whether you're a couple celebrating an anniversary, a group marking a milestone birthday, or an owner exploring what your yacht could become — the promise is the same one our founder made on day one. This will be unlike anything you have experienced on the water.

Home Port

Why St. Petersburg

There is a reason St. Petersburg is known as the Sunshine City. With an average of 361 days of sunshine per year and some of the most beautiful waterways in the Southeast, it is the ideal home port for a luxury yacht charter.

From our marina, the entire Gulf Coast opens up. Head south and you reach the white sand beaches of Egmont Key and the protected waters of Terra Ceia Bay. Turn north and the Anclote Keys offer secluded anchorages surrounded by wildlife. Cross Tampa Bay and you are at the doorstep of Apollo Beach and the Manatee Viewing Center.

For longer voyages, our 600-mile range means the Florida Keys and the Bahamas are well within reach. St. Petersburg gives us access to calm inland waterways for sunset cruises and open Gulf waters for true offshore adventures — all from a single departure point.
