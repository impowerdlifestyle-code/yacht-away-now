How Much Does a Yacht Charter Cost in Tampa Bay?

The complete, transparent pricing breakdown for chartering a luxury yacht in Tampa Bay, Florida — hourly rates, weekend packages, captain fees, and what's actually included. Updated for 2026.

JW
Captain Josh Wilson USCG Licensed · Owner, Yacht Away Now · St. Petersburg, FL · Updated May 2026
Quick Answer

Yacht charters in Tampa Bay start at $400/hour with a 4-hour minimum (plus $50/hr fuel and $100/hr captain). Weekend packages are $2,000 for 4 hours or $2,500 for 5 hours, all-inclusive. Multi-day Bahamas trips start at $5,000. Standard crew gratuity is 18–20%.

Hourly charter rates

Hourly rates are the most flexible way to charter a yacht in Tampa Bay — useful when your group needs less than a full day or wants a custom departure time on a weekday.

That works out to $2,200 for a 4-hour weekday charter all-in (before gratuity). For most groups, the weekend package below is a better value.

What's included (and what isn't)

Always included in the charter price

  • 52ft Marquis Flybridge with 3 decks of luxury living space
  • USCG-licensed captain
  • Professional hospitality crew (deckhand)
  • Ice, bottled water, koozies
  • Premium sound system + Bluetooth/AUX
  • Swim ladder, towels, water floats
  • Air-conditioned interior (1,200 sq ft)
  • Use of all 3 decks (800+ sq ft outdoor space)

Billed separately

  • Crew gratuity (18–20% of charter base; 100% goes to crew)
  • Food & alcohol (BYOB welcome — no glass, Coast Guard rule)
  • Fuel surcharge on hourly charters ($50/hr)
  • Captain fee on hourly charters ($100/hr — already included in weekend packages)
  • Additional dockage / overnight slip fees on multi-day trips

Captain & crew fees

A USCG-licensed captain is required on every charter — there is no bareboat option in our fleet. The captain fee is $100 per hour for hourly bookings and is rolled into the price for weekend packages.

Standard crew gratuity is 18–20% of the charter base, paid in cash or via the booking platform. 100% of gratuity goes directly to the captain and deckhand.

Bahamas & multi-day pricing

The 52ft Marquis Flybridge has a 600-mile range, making it Bahamas-capable from Tampa Bay. Multi-day adventures start at $5,000 and are custom-quoted based on:

  • Number of nights (typical trips run 3–7 days)
  • Fuel for the route (Bahamas crossings typically burn $1,200–$2,000)
  • Dockage and slip fees at destination ports
  • Provisioning and customs fees
  • Additional crew nights

Most multi-day trips land between $5,000 and $15,000 depending on duration and itinerary. Request a custom Bahamas quote →

Hidden costs to watch for

When comparing yacht charter prices in Tampa Bay, here are the line items operators sometimes leave out of the quote:

  • Fuel surcharge — some operators advertise low base rates, then charge $80–$150/hr fuel
  • Captain "service charge" separate from gratuity
  • Cleaning fees ($150–$300) if not included
  • Departure/docking fees for non-standard marinas
  • Mandatory provisioning packages (food/drink minimums)

Yacht Away Now does not charge cleaning, departure, or service-charge fees. Our quotes are flat: boat + crew + fuel = total.

How to get the best price

  • Book weekday slots when possible — hourly rates apply with no minimum surcharge
  • Choose the 4-hour weekend package over the 5-hour if you don't need sunset
  • Book 4–6 weeks ahead for weekends and holidays — last-minute rates rarely come down
  • Bundle multi-day trips — Bahamas itineraries get better per-day rates the longer the booking
  • Avoid peak holidays — Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, NYE all command premium pricing

Frequently asked questions

How much is a 4-hour yacht charter in Tampa Bay?

A 4-hour yacht charter in Tampa Bay costs approximately $2,000 — either as a flat weekend package or $400/hr × 4 hours plus $200 fuel and $400 captain on weekdays. Crew gratuity is additional.

Can I rent a yacht in Tampa Bay for under $1,000?

No — private yacht charters in Tampa Bay realistically start around $2,000 for a 4-hour booking. Smaller boat rentals (pontoons, deck boats) under 30 feet can be found for $400–$800, but those are not yachts and rarely include a captain.

Do yacht charters in Tampa Bay include food?

Most Tampa Bay yacht charters do not include food in the base price — guests bring their own or arrange catering separately. We provide ice, water, and koozies; everything else is BYO.

Is the captain's gratuity required?

Gratuity is not legally required but is strongly customary. 18–20% of the charter base is standard for excellent service, and 100% goes directly to the captain and deckhand who earned it.

How much is a yacht wedding in Tampa Bay?

Yacht wedding packages in Tampa Bay typically run $2,500–$8,000+ depending on duration, guest count, and add-ons (officiant, photographer, catering, decor). Our base 5-hour weekend charter is $2,500; full ceremony and reception packages are quoted custom.

What's the price difference between Tampa Bay and Miami yacht charters?

Tampa Bay yacht charters are typically 30–50% less expensive than equivalent Miami charters. A comparable 52ft yacht with captain runs $4,000–$5,000/day in Miami versus $2,500/day in Tampa Bay, with similar amenities.
