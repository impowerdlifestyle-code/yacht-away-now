How Many People Fit on a Yacht Charter? The Group Size Guide

Passenger limits, per-person math, and how to pick the right boat for your headcount.

July 2026 5 min read
JW
By Captain Josh Wilson
Founder & USCG Master 100-Ton Captain

It's usually the first question after price: "How many people can we bring?" The answer matters more than most guests realize — passenger limits are federal law, not operator preference, and they shape everything from per-person cost to which boat you should book. Here's how it works.

The Number: Up to 13 Guests

Our 52ft Marquis Flybridge hosts up to 13 guests, plus your USCG-licensed captain and crew. That limit is tied to the vessel's federal certification — every charter boat in the country has one, and reputable operators will tell you theirs without hesitation. If a company offers to "squeeze in a few extra," that's a boat you don't want to be on when something goes wrong: exceeding the limit voids insurance and puts everyone aboard at risk.

One detail that surprises planners: children count. A one-year-old occupies a passenger spot the same as an adult, so a group of 11 adults and 3 kids is 14 — one over. Count every human when you're building the invite list.

What 13 People Feels Like on a 52-Footer

Thirteen on a 52ft yacht with three separate decks is comfortable, not crowded. In practice groups spread out naturally — sun-seekers on the bow pads, the conversation cluster on the shaded aft deck, a few up on the flybridge with the captain, someone raiding the galley. At a sandbar stop, half the group is usually in the water anyway. The layout matters more than the length, and a flybridge motor yacht gives you roughly three boats' worth of hangout zones in one hull.

The Per-Person Math

Group size is the lever that turns a luxury into a bargain. At full capacity, a $2,400 weekend charter is about $185 per person; a $2,000 weekday runs roughly $154 per person. At 8 guests you're at $250–300 each — still less than many group dinners on Beach Drive. Full pricing breakdown in our 2026 cost guide.

Smaller Groups and Couples

Don't let the capacity number convince you a charter is only for big groups. Couples book the whole yacht for anniversaries, proposals, and date nights precisely because it's private — no strangers, no shared decks, a crew that fades into the background. Our 3-stateroom yacht Sly Fox (up to 6 guests) is purpose-built for intimate trips and multi-day cruising with an optional chef.

Bigger Than 13?

You have options. Running both yachts together covers 19 guests cruising side by side and rafting up at the sandbar — a spectacular setup for corporate events and milestone birthdays. For larger headcounts we can stagger charters across a day or point you to trusted partners. Tell us your number and we'll design around it: start here or call (727) 609-2248.

Which Boat for Which Headcount

Here's how I match groups to boats when people call. Seven to 13 guests: the 52ft Marquis, full stop. It's the only one certified for that headcount, and the three decks keep a big group from ever feeling stacked. Two to six guests: both boats work, so pick by trip type. Day on the water with a swim stop and a sunset? The Marquis at $500/hr weekdays gives you the most boat for the money. Planning an overnight or a slower multi-day pace? Sly Fox has 3 private staterooms and 2 full baths, sleeps 6, and runs multi-day Gulf Coast trips from $5,000. Fourteen to 19: both yachts together. Twenty or more: call me and we'll figure out staggered charters or a partner vessel.

One number people miss: the boats price differently. Sly Fox runs $650/hr weekdays and $750/hr weekends, so a small group choosing between them should decide on layout and trip style, not assume the smaller passenger count means a smaller bill.

Build the Invite List Like a Captain

The mistake I see most is treating 13 as a target instead of a ceiling. If you invite 13 and get 13 yeses, one surprise plus-one on the dock means someone stays behind, because the limit has no wiggle room. Invite to 13, but know your true count before you book, and remember every child counts as a full passenger. Two more rules from our booking terms: the person booking must be 21 or older, and guests under 18 need a parent or guardian aboard. Life jackets in every size, infant through adult, are already on the boat, so babies and grandparents are genuinely welcome. They just each take a spot.

Sitting at 14 or 15 with a couple of maybes? Don't gamble on a dropout. Book the Marquis for your confirmed 13 and put the overflow question to us early. Adding Sly Fox later depends on her calendar, and weekends go first.

How Headcount Shapes the Itinerary

Group size changes what the best day on the water looks like. Full-boat groups of 10 to 13 get the most out of anchoring time: a run to a sandbar or Shell Key, half the group in the water, music on the deck, long lunch aboard. Shell Key and Egmont Key sit about 30 to 40 minutes apart by yacht, and a 6 to 8 hour charter covers both with real time at each. Smaller groups tend to book the classic 4-hour windows instead. Our typical slots are 10am to 2pm, 2:30 to 6:30pm, and sunset, and for four or six guests the sunset run along Pass-a-Grille is the one I'd pick every time.

Booking Timeline by Group Size

Big groups need runway, and not because of the boat. Coordinating 13 calendars is the slow part. We recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks ahead, earlier for weekends and holidays, and in spring the weekend sunset slots book two to three weeks out. A 50% deposit locks your date, with the balance due 7 days before departure, so you have time to collect from the group. Couples and small groups have more flexibility: last-minute weekday availability is often possible, and one phone call answers it.

Yacht Charter Group Size — FAQ

How many people can go on a yacht charter?

Our 52ft Marquis Flybridge hosts up to 13 guests plus the captain and crew. Passenger limits are set by federal regulations tied to each vessel's certification — every legitimate charter operator has a hard cap, and it's worth being suspicious of any that won't state theirs.

Do children count toward the passenger limit on a boat?

Yes. Coast Guard passenger limits count every guest regardless of age — a toddler occupies a spot the same as an adult. We carry life jackets in all sizes, including infant and child sizes, for every charter.

What if my group is bigger than 13 people?

Two options: our second yacht Sly Fox adds capacity for 6 more guests cruising alongside, or we can help you split into two staggered charters. For corporate events with larger headcounts, call us — we've coordinated multi-vessel days before.

Is a yacht charter worth it for a small group or a couple?

Absolutely — some of our best charters are two people. A weekday 4-hour charter at $500/hour gives a couple the entire 52ft yacht, crew, and a private sunset for $2,000, which is why it's become a go-to for proposals and milestone anniversaries.

How far in advance should a big group book?

Book 2 to 4 weeks ahead, and earlier for weekends and holidays. In spring, weekend sunset slots book two to three weeks out. A 50% deposit locks your date, with the balance due 7 days before departure, which gives you time to collect from the group.

Are there age requirements for a yacht charter?

The person booking must be 21 or older, and guests under 18 need a parent or guardian aboard. Children and families are welcome, every guest counts toward the 13-passenger limit regardless of age, and life jackets in all sizes including infant and child are on the boat.
