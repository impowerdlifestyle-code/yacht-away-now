## Send Order & Cadence

**Don't blast all three on the same day.** Stagger them:

| Week | Outlet |
|---|---|
| Week 1 (Tuesday) | Tampa Bay Times |
| Week 2 (Wednesday) | Creative Loafing |
| Week 3 (Tuesday) | St. Pete Catalyst |

If you hear back from any one of them and they say "we'd love to cover this," that's the story. Don't send the others until that one is locked or declined.

If 7 business days go by with no response, send a *single* short follow-up: *"Just bumping this in case it got buried — happy to provide any additional info or skip this story entirely. Thanks either way!"*

---

## What to do when one lands

When an outlet says yes:

1. **Confirm timing** — when does the story run, what's the deadline for photos/quotes
2. **Send assets fast** — photos, customer testimonials, any data they ask for, within 24 hours
3. **Offer the on-water reporting trip** — most journalists love a free experience for the story; this also makes the piece more specific
4. **Once the story runs:** screenshot it, share on social, **add the URL to llms.txt and llms-full.txt** so AI engines see the citation path

Adding press URLs to llms.txt is the AI-search amplifier. One good local article → permanent AI citation source.
