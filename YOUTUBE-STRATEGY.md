## 5. Equipment Recommendations (Budget-Friendly)

### Essential (Under $50 Total)

| Item | Est. Cost | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Your phone** | $0 | iPhone 13+ or Samsung Galaxy S21+ shoots incredible 4K video. Use landscape mode ALWAYS. |
| **Phone gimbal** | ~$30 | DJI Osmo Mobile SE or Hohem iSteady V2. Critical for smooth walk-through shots on a moving boat. Eliminates shaky footage completely. |
| **Waterproof phone case** | ~$15 | Universal waterproof pouch (e.g., JOTO or Mpow). Allows underwater filming for snorkeling shots without risking your phone. Test it in a sink before the ocean. |

### Recommended (Optional Upgrades)

| Item | Est. Cost | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **GoPro Hero** | ~$200–350 | Best for underwater, action, and mounted shots. Suction mount it to the hull for unique angles. The wide-angle lens captures the full yacht experience. |
| **Mini tripod** | ~$15 | For timelapse shots. Mount phone on flybridge rail or flat surfaces. Joby GorillaPod or similar flexible tripod. |
| **Drone** | ~$300–750 | DJI Mini 4K or Mini 4 Pro. This is the single biggest upgrade for yacht content. Aerial shots of the yacht on open water are unmatched. Check FAA regulations for your area. |
| **Wireless lavalier mic** | ~$25–50 | For testimonial and narration audio. Wind on the water destroys built-in phone mics. Rode Wireless ME or MAYBESTA brand on Amazon. |
| **Portable battery pack** | ~$20 | 10,000mAh minimum. Filming all day on a charter will kill your phone battery. |

### Audio & Music

- **Natural audio is your friend.** Waves, engine hum, wind, seagulls, and guest laughter all add authenticity. Capture it.
- **When you need background music, use royalty-free sources:**
  - **YouTube Audio Library** (free, built into YouTube Studio)
  - **Epidemic Sound** (~$15/month, huge library, no copyright strikes)
  - **Artlist** (~$10/month, cinematic music and SFX)
  - **Pixabay Music** (free, no attribution required)
- **Music style guide for Yacht Away Now content:**
  - Sunset cruises: Lo-fi, chill house, acoustic — relaxed and warm
  - Adventure content (Egmont Key, Bahamas): Upbeat indie, tropical house — energetic but not aggressive
  - Testimonials: Soft acoustic or light piano underneath — don't overpower the voices
  - Timelapses: Ambient, chillwave, or cinematic — let the visuals lead

### Filming Tips Specific to Yacht Content

1. **Shoot during golden hour whenever possible.** The hour before sunset makes everything look cinematic with zero effort.
2. **Wipe your lens constantly.** Salt spray is your enemy. Keep a microfiber cloth in your pocket.
3. **Film in 4K, deliver in 1080p.** 4K gives you room to crop and stabilize in post without losing quality.
4. **Get low angles.** Shooting from deck level looking up at the flybridge or across the water makes the yacht look massive and dramatic.
5. **Always film more than you think you need.** You can cut a 2-hour shoot into a 3-minute video, but you cannot recreate a moment you didn't capture.
6. **Stabilize on the water.** Even with a gimbal, brace yourself against something solid when filming underway. Widen your stance like you're surfing.
7. **Clean the yacht before filming.** Coil lines, stow fenders, wipe surfaces. A clean yacht on camera signals professionalism.
