Fireworks on the Water

The Best Seats in Tampa Bay

There is no better way to watch fireworks than from the water. While thousands of people crowd the shoreline fighting for a spot, you and up to 12 guests are anchored in the middle of Tampa Bay with a 360-degree, unobstructed view of the entire fireworks display — plus a private 52ft luxury yacht as your floating VIP lounge.

Tampa Bay hosts some of the most spectacular fireworks shows in Florida, and every major display is visible from the water. July 4th fireworks launch from multiple locations around the bay — St. Pete Pier, Vinoy Park, Clearwater Beach, and Tampa's Channelside district. From our yacht, you can see multiple shows simultaneously. New Year's Eve brings midnight fireworks over downtown St. Petersburg and Tampa, with the city skylines as a backdrop. Gasparilla combines the daytime pirate invasion parade with evening fireworks over Hillsborough Bay — a uniquely Tampa experience.

Our captain positions the yacht in the optimal viewing location based on wind direction, crowd patterns, and the specific launch sites for each event. You get front-row seats without the traffic, parking, or crowds. Bring your own food, drinks, and decorations — crank up the premium stereo, connect to our Starlink WiFi to livestream the moment, and celebrate in true VIP fashion.

Book early. Fireworks charters are our most popular special event bookings and sell out 4-6 weeks in advance. July 4th and New Year's Eve dates typically book out by early June and mid-November respectively. A 50% deposit locks in your date.

July 4th — Multiple Shows

See fireworks from St. Pete Pier, Vinoy Park, Clearwater, and Tampa simultaneously. Our captain anchors for the best multi-show viewing angle on the bay.

Common Questions

Fireworks Charter FAQ

How early should I book a fireworks charter?

Book 4-6 weeks in advance. July 4th charters typically sell out by early June. New Year's Eve dates fill up by mid-November. Gasparilla charters should be booked by early January. A 50% deposit locks in your date.

Which fireworks shows can you see from the yacht?

Tampa Bay hosts fireworks for July 4th (St. Pete Pier, Vinoy Park, Clearwater, Tampa Channelside), New Year's Eve (downtown St. Pete and Tampa), and Gasparilla (Hillsborough Bay). From the yacht, you can often see multiple shows simultaneously.

Do you anchor or cruise during fireworks?

We anchor in the optimal viewing location before the show starts. Our captain positions the yacht based on wind direction, crowd patterns, and launch sites. You get a stable, front-row viewing experience without the rocking of cruising.

Is it safe to be on the water at night?

Absolutely. Our yacht is equipped with full navigation lights, GPS, radar, and VHF radio. Your captain is a USCG-licensed captain with extensive night navigation experience on Tampa Bay. Safety equipment including life jackets for all guests is always aboard.

Can we bring sparklers or fireworks on the yacht?

No — personal fireworks, sparklers, and any open flame items are not permitted aboard for safety reasons. You will have the best fireworks show in Tampa Bay right in front of you from the comfort of the yacht.
