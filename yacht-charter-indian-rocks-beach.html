Indian Rocks Beach Charters

Your Private Gulf Coast Escape

Indian Rocks Beach is one of the Gulf Coast's best-kept secrets — a quiet, laid-back beach community between Clearwater and St. Petersburg that offers some of the most beautiful waterfront scenery in Pinellas County. And with Yacht Away Now, you can experience it all from the deck of a private 52ft luxury yacht.

Our charters serve the entire Indian Rocks Beach corridor, including neighboring Belleair Beach, Indian Shores, Redington Shores, and Madeira Beach. Departing from St. Petersburg, we cruise north along the Intracoastal Waterway, passing through calm, protected waters with views of waterfront mansions, mangrove islands, and the stunning Narrows — a scenic stretch of the ICW between Indian Rocks and Clearwater.

Popular charter experiences from Indian Rocks Beach include sunset cruises along the Gulf-facing shoreline, sandbar stops at Caladesi Island (consistently rated one of America's best beaches), dolphin watching in Clearwater Harbor, and full-day adventures combining multiple stops along the barrier island chain.

Indian Rocks Beach is also a popular departure point for special occasions — birthdays, anniversaries, bachelorette parties, and family reunions. The area's calm waters and stunning Gulf sunsets create the perfect backdrop for celebrations. Our yacht accommodates up to 13 guests with 3 decks, premium stereo, Starlink WiFi, full galley kitchen, and professional captain and crew.

Intracoastal Waterway

Cruise the protected ICW waters between Indian Rocks Beach and Clearwater — calm, scenic, and perfect for guests who prefer smooth sailing.

Caladesi Island

Visit one of America's top-rated beaches, accessible only by boat. Pristine white sand, clear water, and no crowds — the ultimate Florida island experience.

Gulf Sunset Views

Clearwater Connection

Common Questions

Indian Rocks Beach Charter FAQ

Do you depart from Indian Rocks Beach?

We depart from our home port in St. Petersburg, FL. Indian Rocks Beach is approximately 30-40 minutes by yacht from our dock, cruising north along the scenic Intracoastal Waterway. The cruise up is part of the experience.

Can we visit Caladesi Island on our charter?

Yes — Caladesi Island State Park, consistently rated one of America's best beaches, is accessible by boat and is a popular stop on Indian Rocks Beach area charters. The island has pristine white sand, clear water, and a nature trail.

Are the waters calm near Indian Rocks Beach?

Yes — the Intracoastal Waterway between St. Petersburg and Indian Rocks Beach is protected from Gulf swells, making for very smooth cruising. If you prefer calm water, the ICW route is ideal.

What is the best season for an Indian Rocks Beach charter?

Year-round chartering is available. March through October is peak season with warmer water and longer days. Winter months (November-February) offer cooler temperatures, less crowding, and beautiful clear skies.

How do I book a charter near Indian Rocks Beach?

Call (727) 609-2248 or fill out our online booking form at yachtawaynow.com/contact. A 50% deposit locks in your date. We recommend booking 2-3 weeks in advance, especially for weekends.
