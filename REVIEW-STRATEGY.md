## 7. Review Milestone Goals

| Milestone | Target Date | Action |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **65 reviews** (current) | Now | Baseline — begin executing this strategy |
| **75 reviews** | April 14, 2026 (2 weeks) | Text every charter guest using SMS template. Josh sends personally. |
| **100 reviews** | May 12, 2026 (6 weeks) | Add full email follow-up sequence (morning-after + 5-day nudge). Automate via CRM or email tool. |
| **150 reviews** | June 30, 2026 (3 months) | Add physical review cards with QR code. Hand to every guest at disembarkation. |
| **200 reviews** | September 30, 2026 (6 months) | Maintain cadence across all channels. Evaluate response rate and optimize templates. |

### Key Metrics to Track
- **Review request send rate:** Are texts/emails going out after every charter?
- **Conversion rate:** What % of requests result in a review?
- **Average rating:** Track weekly to catch any service issues early
- **Response rate:** Respond to 100% of reviews within 24 hours

### Weekly Rhythm
- **Monday:** Respond to any new reviews from the weekend
- **Wednesday:** Publish one GBP post (rotate through the 4 templates above)
- **Friday:** Check that all charter guests from the week received their SMS/email sequence
