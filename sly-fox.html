What's Included

Yours for the run of the Gulf Coast. Add a chef, add a captain, or take the helm yourself.

Four Real Bedrooms

Three queen-bed master suites and a twin-bed cabin, each with its own door, climate control, and ensuite or adjacent full bath. Linens, pillows, and bath towels stocked. The master suites are sized for couples; the twin cabin sleeps two with room to spare.

Optional Chef & Captain

A licensed captain and a private chef can be arranged on request. The captain handles cruising, marina coordination, and excursion routing. The chef provisions and prepares breakfasts, lunches, and dinners aboard — coordinated through our concierge before you arrive.

Gulf Coast Itineraries

The yacht runs multi-day Gulf Coast itineraries from St. Petersburg — anchor overnight at Shell Key or Egmont Key, run south to Anna Maria Island and Sarasota, or head out to the offshore reefs for a fishing weekend. We help plan provisioning and route based on your group, weather window, and how much ground you want to cover.

Overview

A Floating Escape on the Gulf

Wake to a Gulf Coast sunrise, run out to a sandbar or quiet anchorage for the afternoon, then settle in for the night with the stars overhead. Sly Fox is a real home on the water with four bedrooms, four and a half bathrooms, and the room to entertain six guests in serious comfort. The boat anchors the night, the chef cooks dinner if you want one, and the captain handles every detail of the cruise the next morning.

Three queen-bed master suites and a twin-bed cabin mean every guest gets a real room — no folding sofas, no shared bunks. The aft deck, salon, and flybridge each accommodate the full party at once, which makes the yacht equally good for a multi-couple long weekend, a family island-hopping trip, or a quiet retreat for two with the run of the whole vessel.
