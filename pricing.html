Weekday vs. Weekend: What Will I Pay?

One transparent all-inclusive hourly rate — $500/hr Monday through Thursday and $600/hr Friday through Sunday, 4-hour minimum, with captain, crew, and fuel included. A 4-hour charter is exactly $2,000 on a weekday or $2,400 on a weekend; 5 hours is $2,500 or $3,000. If your dates are flexible, weekdays are the better deal — same yacht, same crew, same route.

Bahamas & Multi-Day Trip Pricing

Multi-day Bahamas and Florida Keys charters start at $9,500 and are quoted custom based on duration, destination, fuel for the offshore crossing, dockage at island marinas, and provisioning. A typical 3-day Bimini run runs $9,500–$13,000 all-in for a group of 6–8; longer Exumas trips and Key West voyages quote higher. The yacht has a 600-mile range and full cabin accommodations for overnight comfort. Call (727) 609-2248 for a same-day custom quote.

What's Included in Every Charter?

Every Yacht Away Now charter includes the full 52ft Marquis Flybridge (all 3 decks, 1,200+ sq ft cabin, 800+ sq ft deck), your USCG-licensed captain at the helm, premium audio across all decks, Starlink WiFi, ice and cooler space, USCG-rated life jackets for every guest, and customizable routing within the day's itinerary. BYOB and BYO food are welcome on every charter — we provide ice and cooler space; glass bottles transferred to plastic dockside (Coast Guard rule). Captain gratuity (18–20% of base) is at guest discretion and is not included.

Common Questions

Yacht Charter Pricing FAQ

What's included in the charter price?

Everything — every charter is one all-inclusive rate with captain, crew, and fuel included and no hidden fees: $500/hr on weekdays (Mon–Thu) and $600/hr on weekends (Fri–Sun), with a 4-hour minimum. All charters include full vessel access across 3 decks, 2 cabins, premium stereo with Bluetooth, Starlink WiFi, and capacity for up to 13 guests.

Do I need to bring my own food and drinks?

Yes — all charters are BYOB. You're welcome to bring your own food, drinks, and alcohol aboard. There's a full galley kitchen, refrigerator, and plenty of cooler space. Many guests pick up catering from local St. Petersburg restaurants before boarding. Need suggestions? Just ask when you book.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks in advance, especially for weekends and holidays. Peak season (March through October) fills up fast. For holidays like July 4th, Gasparilla, and New Year's Eve, book 4–6 weeks ahead to secure your date. A 50% deposit locks in your charter date.

What if I need to change my date?

We reschedule flexibly within available dates. If your captain calls off a charter for weather or safety — including a named storm forecast within 72 hours — you choose any open future date, and your booking stays valid for 12 months. Your safety is always our top priority.

Can I customize the route or itinerary?

Absolutely. Every charter is private and fully customizable. Popular routes include sunset cruises past the St. Pete Pier and Vinoy, island hops to Egmont Key and Shell Key, sandbar stops at Fort De Soto, and passes under the Skyway Bridge. Your captain will work with you to plan the perfect route based on weather, tides, and your preferences.

Is gratuity expected for the captain and crew?

Gratuity is not included in the charter price and is entirely at your discretion. If you had a great experience, most guests tip 10–15% of the charter total. You can tip in cash at the end of your trip or add it when completing payment.
