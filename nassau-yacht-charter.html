Overview

A Floating Escape in Nassau

Wake to the Bahamian sun, cross to a private cay for the afternoon, then dock for the night at Atlantis Marina with the resort buzzing one slip away. This is a liveaboard yacht — a real home on the water with four bedrooms, four and a half bathrooms, and the room to entertain six guests in serious comfort. The boat anchors the night, the chef cooks dinner if you want one, and the captain handles every detail of the cruise the next morning.

Three queen-bed master suites and a twin-bed cabin mean every guest gets a real room — no folding sofas, no shared bunks. The aft deck, salon, and flybridge each accommodate the full party at once, which makes the yacht equally good for a multi-couple long weekend, a family island-hopping trip, or a quiet retreat for two with the run of the whole vessel.

What's Included

A liveaboard yacht with the run of the Bahamas. Add a chef, add a captain, or take the helm yourself.

Four Real Bedrooms

Three queen-bed master suites and a twin-bed cabin, each with its own door, climate control, and ensuite or adjacent full bath. Linens, pillows, and bath towels stocked. The master suites are sized for couples; the twin cabin sleeps two with room to spare.

Optional Chef & Captain

A licensed captain and a private chef can be arranged on request. The captain handles cruising, marina coordination, and excursion routing. The chef provisions and prepares breakfasts, lunches, and dinners aboard — coordinated through our concierge before you arrive.

Atlantis Marina Docking

The yacht is permitted to dock at Atlantis Marina on Paradise Island — full access to the resort's pools, casino, dining, and beach club from your slip. Docking fees apply per night and are billed separately. Alternative cays and Nassau anchorages are available for quieter nights.
