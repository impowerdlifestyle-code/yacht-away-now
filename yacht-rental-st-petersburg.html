What "Rental" Means on This Boat

If you typed "yacht rental St Petersburg" hoping to take the keys and go, here is the honest answer up front: we do not offer bareboat rentals. A 52-foot twin-engine flybridge with 13 people aboard is not a pontoon, and neither our insurer nor the Coast Guard would let it leave the dock without a licensed master at the helm. Every rental comes with Captain Josh Wilson or one of his crew driving.

In practice that works in your favor. You rent the whole yacht by the hour, decide where it goes, and skip every chore that comes with a bareboat: no fuel dock, no chart plotter, no docking a large boat in a crosswind with an audience, no scrubbing it down at the end. You step off at the same pier you stepped onto.

Availability

When You Can Get the Boat

We run one yacht per booking, so there is a single slot per departure and it goes to whoever confirms first. Weekend afternoons and sunset departures from March through October fill two to four weeks out; Gasparilla, July 4th and New Year's Eve fill four to six weeks out. Weekday mornings are the easiest to get, and a last-minute Tuesday is often possible if you call.

If the Marquis is spoken for, or your group would rather have staterooms than a dance floor, the second yacht in the fleet, Sly Fox, rents at $650 an hour Mon-Thu and $750 Fri-Sun and sleeps six for overnights. Both boats leave from the same dock at Maximo Marina, 38th Way S, St. Petersburg 33711, a few minutes from the Skyway and the open Gulf.

St. Petersburg Yacht Rental FAQ

Common Questions

Can I rent the yacht without a captain?

No. Every Yacht Away Now rental is captained by owner Josh Wilson, a USCG Master, or his crew. The 52ft Marquis is a commercially inspected vessel and cannot be rented bareboat. The captain is included in the hourly rate, not billed separately.

What does a yacht rental in St. Petersburg cost per hour?

$500 an hour Monday through Thursday and $600 an hour Friday through Sunday, with a four-hour minimum. That covers the captain, crew, fuel and ice. Four hours on a weekday is $2,000; five hours on a weekend is $3,000. Gratuity of 10 to 15 percent is optional.

Do you charge extra for fuel or cleaning?

No. Fuel, cleaning, ice and crew are inside the hourly rate, and there is no platform service fee because you are booking directly with the owner. The only cost that is not included is captain gratuity, which is at your discretion.

How far in advance should I book a yacht rental in St. Pete?

Two to four weeks for a weekend or sunset slot between March and October, four to six weeks for Gasparilla, July 4th or New Year's Eve. Weekday mornings can often be booked within a few days. A 50 percent deposit holds the date and the balance is due seven days before departure.

Where do I pick up the yacht?

Both yachts leave from Maximo Marina at 38th Way S, St. Petersburg, FL 33711, on the south side of the city near the Skyway. Arrive about 15 minutes before your departure time; the crew will load your drinks into iced coolers and move anything in glass into plastic.
