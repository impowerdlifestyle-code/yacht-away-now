Everything you need to know about chartering with Yacht Away Now.

Our 52ft Marquis Flybridge takes up to 13 guests, and children count toward that limit. Your group has the whole boat: the flybridge, the aft deck and the climate-controlled salon, plus 2 cabins below. Life jackets are aboard for every guest, and kids 6 and under wear one at all times.
Every charter is one all-inclusive rate. Your USCG-licensed captain, crew and fuel are part of the price, with no hidden fees.
