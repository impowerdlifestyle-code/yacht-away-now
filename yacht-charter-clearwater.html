Why Clearwater

Clearwater's Best Kept Secret

Clearwater Beach is consistently ranked among the top beaches in the United States — and the best way to experience it is from the water. Whether you're looking for a private boat rental in Clearwater or a full-service yacht charter, we give you access to the most breathtaking stretches of Clearwater's coastline, far from the crowded shore.

Departing from our St. Petersburg dock, it's a short and scenic cruise north through the Intracoastal Waterway to reach Clearwater's turquoise waters. Along the way, you'll pass stunning waterfront estates, pristine mangrove islands, and the iconic Clearwater Pass — the gateway to the Gulf of Mexico's open waters.

Destinations

Where We'll Take You

From our St. Petersburg dock, Clearwater's finest destinations are just a cruise away. Here's what awaits you on the water.

Clearwater Beach

America's top-rated beach is even more stunning from the water. Anchor offshore, swim in crystal-clear Gulf waters, and take in panoramic views of the Clearwater skyline. The sugar-white sand stretches for miles.

Caladesi Island State Park

One of the few completely natural barrier islands left in Florida. Accessible only by boat, Caladesi offers untouched beaches, nature trails, and a kayak trail through mangrove tunnels. A true hidden paradise.

Sand Key & The Gulf

Sand Key sits just south of Clearwater Pass, offering calm waters and a serene atmosphere perfect for anchoring. From here, head through the pass into the open Gulf for dolphin encounters and legendary sunsets.

Your captain's Tip — The Clearwater Pass Window

Clearwater Pass is one of the narrower inlets on the west coast and the current rips through it on outgoing tide. We time departures to leave the marina an hour before slack low water so we transit the pass on a fair current — quieter water, no standing waves at the seawall, easier for guests on the bow. Caladesi Island is a 25-minute run north and ranks consistently in the country's top ten beach lists; Honeymoon Island is another five minutes past that. Both have day-use anchorages with sandy bottom in 6-8 feet of water. For sunset, hold offshore of Sand Key and you'll get the unobstructed Gulf horizon without bridge or pier in the frame.

A Sample 4-Hour Clearwater Itinerary

  1. 11:00 a.m. — Boarding at Maximo Marina, St. Petersburg. Safety briefing, drinks set out, sunscreen on.
  2. 11:30 a.m. — Cruise north along the Intracoastal toward Clearwater Pass — Belleair Beach, Indian Shores, and Madeira Beach to starboard.
  3. 12:30 p.m. — Transit Clearwater Pass on fair current, anchor off Caladesi Island for swim, paddle, or beach landing.
  4. 1:30 p.m. — Optional run north to Honeymoon Island state park, or back through the pass to Sand Key for sunset positioning.
  5. 2:30 p.m. — Return south to Maximo Marina. Total run: ~45 nautical miles. All fuel included on weekend packages.

Clearwater Charter FAQ

Questions, Answered

How much does a yacht charter in Clearwater cost?

Our 52ft Marquis Flybridge is all-inclusive with captain, crew, and fuel: $500 per hour on weekdays and $600 per hour on weekends, with a 4-hour minimum. A 4-hour weekend charter for the Clearwater coast is $2,400.

Do you depart from Clearwater Beach?

We depart from our home marina in St. Petersburg, about 40 minutes from Clearwater Beach with free parking. That means no Memorial Causeway beach traffic, no crowded municipal marina, and faster access to open Gulf water.

Can we visit Caladesi Island on a charter?

Yes. Caladesi Island State Park is one of the last untouched barrier islands in Florida and is only reachable by boat, which makes it a favorite stop on Clearwater-area charters along with Sand Key and Clearwater Pass.

How many people can join a Clearwater yacht charter?

Up to 13 guests. The 52ft Marquis has three decks with over 1,200 sq ft of cabin space, a full galley, and a premium stereo, so groups spread out comfortably. Charters are BYOB.

Do you offer sunset cruises for Clearwater?

Yes — the Gulf-facing Clearwater coast produces the sunsets Pier 60 celebrates nightly, and we time departures to put you on open water for golden hour. See our Clearwater sunset cruise for routes and pricing.
